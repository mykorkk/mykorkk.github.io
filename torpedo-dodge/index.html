Privacy Policy — Torpedo Dodge

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Olbia Games ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game Torpedo Dodge (the "App") available on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your choices.

By using Torpedo Dodge, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Torpedo Dodge does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal contact information.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

a) Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App displays interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect: advertising identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID), IP address, device information (model, OS version), and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks). Where required by law (see "Your Choices" below), ads are only personalized after you provide consent.

b) In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
The App offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase) processed entirely through Google Play's billing system, using Unity Gaming Services / Unity IAP as the technical purchasing layer. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled directly by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which we use to grant the corresponding in-game reward.

c) Locally Stored Game Data
Your progress (total coins, high score, unlocked/selected ships, and whether you've purchased "Remove Ads") is stored locally on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs storage. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party, and is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Display and measure advertising, and personalize ads where you have consented
  • Process in-app purchases and deliver the purchased content
  • Save your local game progress
  • Maintain and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information directly, and we do not operate any of our own analytics or tracking servers — all third-party data collection is performed by Google (AdMob and Google Play Billing/Unity Gaming Services) under their own privacy policies, linked below.

4. Your Choices (EEA/UK Users — GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the App shows a consent form the first time you play, asking whether you allow personalized advertising, in line with GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can decline personalization and still use the App and see ads (non-personalized).

You can also manage or reset your ad personalization preferences at any time from your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (Android).

Under GDPR you have the right to: access, correct, or delete data held about you by our third-party processors; object to or restrict certain processing; data portability; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not directly hold personal data on our own servers, these requests are generally best directed to Google via their own privacy tools, or to us at the contact below and we will assist.

5. Your Choices (California/US Users — CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Ad personalization through AdMob may be considered "sharing" under this law. You can opt out via the in-app consent tool (where shown) or via your device's ad settings described above.

6. Children's Privacy

Torpedo Dodge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below so we can address it.

7. Data Security

We rely on Google Play's and Google AdMob's infrastructure to handle payment and advertising data securely. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we do not independently store sensitive personal data on our own servers.
